Privacy policy

Updated on 1 November 2026

What data it handles

Weight, sex, daily goal and water logs. These are the only data Hydrox asks for, and they are used only to calculate how much water you need in a day and to show you where you stand.

Where it lives

In the app's database, on your iPhone. Hydrox has no server, no account and transmits nothing: there is no place where your data could leak out.

No third parties

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDK. The only outside connection is the subscription purchase, handled by Apple.

Deleting everything

Uninstalling the app makes the data disappear with it, because no copy exists anywhere else. From the logs you can delete individual entries at any time.
