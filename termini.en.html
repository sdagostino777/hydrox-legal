What Hydrox is

Hydrox is a helper for remembering to drink. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose and it does not replace a doctor's advice.

Hydrox PRO

The subscription unlocks your history with statistics. You pay through your App Store account and it renews automatically until you cancel, which you do in iOS settings. Cancelling stops the renewal but does not refund the period already started.

Free stays free

The progress ring, the quick log, the reminders and the data export do not require the subscription and never will.
