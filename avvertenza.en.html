Not medical advice

The daily goal comes from your weight with a general formula and can be changed by hand. It does not take illness, medication, pregnancy, physical activity or climate into account. If your doctor tells you otherwise, follow that.

When to stop

Drinking a lot of water in a short time can be dangerous. Do not use this app to force amounts beyond your thirst, and if in doubt talk to a professional.
